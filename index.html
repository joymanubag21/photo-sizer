Printer settings

    Getting it to the printer

    • Canon SELPHY Photo Layout app every setting
      On the printer: Wi-Fi settings → Connection Settings → Other → Direct Connection → On, then note the password. On your phone: Settings → Wi-Fi → join the SELPHY's network. Then pick the sheet from inside the Canon app, not from Photos.

      Faster: Wi-Fi settings → Display Wi-Fi status shows a QR code.
    • SD card no network
      Copy the sheets onto a card and choose them on the printer's own screen. Nothing depends on Wi-Fi.
    • AirPrint no scaling control
      Photos → Share → Print. Only finds the printer when both are on the same 2.4 GHz network. No AirPrint Printers Found usually means the printer is on a different network, or still in direct-connection mode.
