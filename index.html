    Getting it to the printer

    • Canon SELPHY Photo Layout app every setting
      On the printer: Wi-Fi settings → Connection Settings → Other → Direct Connection → On, then note the password it gives you. On your phone: Settings → Wi-Fi → join the SELPHY's network and enter that password. Then pick the sheet from inside the Canon app — not from Photos.

      Faster: Wi-Fi settings → Display Wi-Fi status shows a QR code that connects you without typing anything.
    • SD card no network
      Copy the sheets onto a card and choose them on the printer's own screen. The layout and borderless settings live in the printer menu, so nothing depends on Wi-Fi.
    • AirPrint no scaling control
      Photos → Share → Print. Only finds the printer when both are on the same 2.4 GHz network — the SELPHY has no 5 GHz radio, and guest networks block the discovery it needs. No AirPrint Printers Found almost always means the printer is on a different network, or still in direct-connection mode.

    Sheets come out as JPEG at 1874 × 1402 px — 400 dpi on a 119 × 89 mm sheet. Cropping is done against the real frame geometry — mm on paper, px at 400 dpi — so what you see in the proof is exactly what the dye-sub head lays down. The low-res flag fires when a frame would be fed fewer source pixels than the format needs.
